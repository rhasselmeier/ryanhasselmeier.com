About

I’m Ryan Hasselmeier—a husband, father, native Texan, and proud American.

I’ve spent most of my career building and operating businesses, solving problems, and helping turn ideas into practical results. My work has crossed business advisory, technology, travel, media, custom products, and new ventures.

I tend to learn by building. That might mean improving a business process, developing a website, testing a new use for AI, designing a custom computer system, or creating a space-colony simulation game.

This site is a home for the businesses, projects, interests, and convictions that make up my work and life.

Builder by nature

I have always been drawn to complicated problems and new possibilities. I like taking an idea apart, understanding how it works, and turning it into something useful.

That instinct runs through nearly everything I do: advising businesses, developing digital products, exploring practical uses for AI, building computer systems, and creating Mission Galaxy. The subjects change, but the approach does not—I learn, build, test, and improve.

AI is part of that work, but it is a tool rather than the story. I’m most interested in where it can help people do better work, reduce unnecessary friction, and turn good ideas into finished results.
